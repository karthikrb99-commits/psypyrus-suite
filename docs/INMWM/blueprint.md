# Indian National Mental Wellness Mission (INMWM)
## Master Blueprint

This document is the consolidated national reform framework developed across the project chat.

### Core themes

- psychology education reform
- protected titles and chartered psychology framework
- bridge pathways for existing BA/BSc/MA/MSc/MPhil/PhD cohorts
- multiple intakes and on-demand certification where appropriate
- recruitment and public-service cadres
- school, workplace, hospital, court, defence, and community deployment
- task-sharing and supervised community models
- OPD insurance and fee-band regulation
- therapy-abuse reporting and whistle-blower protections
- HIPAA-level privacy architecture in Indian context
- social-media misinformation moderation
- digital interoperability and AI governance
- governance, budgeting, KPI dashboards, and implementation roadmap

### Design principle

The Mission should be treated as the policy and standards layer that informs product design, clinical workflows, records, assessments, referrals, workforce planning, and collaboration models across the Papyrus / PsyPyrus ecosystem.

### Bridge to CareerOS

CareerOS should be treated as the career-intelligence and workforce-planning companion framework. It can consume INMWM role definitions, recruitment pathways, competency ladders, training stages, and public-service career architectures to support:

- student pathway planning
- bridge-program guidance
- specialty selection
- workforce forecasting
- public-service career mapping
- skill-gap analysis
- national recruitment readiness

### Operational relationship

- **PsyPyrus / Papyrus**: clinical operating system, documentation, assessment, care workflow, and service delivery layer
- **INMWM**: policy, governance, education, licensing, and national standards layer
- **CareerOS**: career guidance, workforce planning, pathway mapping, and opportunity discovery layer

### Notes

The mission framework was written as a long-form national white paper and should be expanded into modular annexes, SOPs, and technical standards before implementation.
