# INMWM ↔ CareerOS Bridge

CareerOS should function as the workforce-intelligence and career-pathway layer that sits alongside the mental-health operating system.

## Shared concepts

- role taxonomy
- specialist tracks
- public-service cadres
- bridge pathways for existing learners
- on-demand certification where appropriate
- multiple intakes and modular progression
- recruitment readiness
- training ROI and career planning
- competency mapping
- workflow handoffs between education, practice, and employment

## How it fits

- **INMWM** defines the national policy and regulatory architecture.
- **PsyPyrus** implements the clinical workflow, documentation, assessments, and care collaboration layer.
- **CareerOS** translates the policy architecture into career discovery, pathway planning, and workforce movement logic.

## Suggested CareerOS integrations

- school-to-profession pathway navigator
- bridge-pathway planner
- specialty comparison matrix
- public-service job map for psychologists
- exam and intake calendar tracker
- ROI and training-cost helper
- competency and licensure milestone tracker
