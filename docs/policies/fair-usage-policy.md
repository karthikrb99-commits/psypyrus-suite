# Fair Usage Policy

**Project:** PsyPyrus Suite / Papyrus Ecosystem

**Purpose:** Define acceptable use, preserve service integrity, protect clinical safety, and support the Indian National Mental Wellness Mission-aligned public-good framing of the project.

## 1. Policy Statement

PsyPyrus is intended to function as open mental-health infrastructure: interoperable, auditable, low-friction, and safe for clinical and public-benefit workflows. Fair use means using the platform in a way that preserves access for others, respects clinical workflows, avoids abuse, and does not compromise privacy, security, or provenance.

This policy supplements, and does not replace, the repository licence, security policy, code of conduct, and any separate trademark or commercial terms.

## 2. Who This Applies To

This policy applies to:

- practitioners and supervised trainees
- patients and caregivers
- hospitals, NGOs, universities, and public programs
- developers, integrators, and API consumers
- organizations running internal or external deployments
- downstream users of derivative builds, ports, or forks

## 3. Acceptable Use

Allowed use includes:

- clinical documentation, intake, MSE, assessment, and treatment planning
- patient onboarding, scheduling, reminders, follow-up, and care coordination
- supervised clinical workflows with audit trails
- research and analytics under consent, governance, and de-identification rules
- reasonable API usage aligned with the current plan or deployment limits
- deployment in low-resource settings where the use case is consistent with public mental-health access
- educational and training use for psychology, psychiatry, social work, nursing, and allied mental-health roles

## 4. Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- bypass authentication, authorization, quotas, safety checks, or audit logs
- scrape, bulk export, or mirror data without permission
- use the platform for spam, fraud, impersonation, coercion, harassment, or phishing
- upload malware, exploit payloads, or harmful automation
- use bots to overload services, evade throttles, or degrade availability
- fabricate clinical records, assessments, or outcomes
- present AI output as a final diagnosis or substitute for qualified professional judgment
- access records without a legitimate care, supervision, or administrative reason
- attempt to conceal provenance, authorship, or required notices in derivative deployments
- imply governmental endorsement, official mission status, or institutional affiliation without written proof

## 5. Fair Use Triggers

The following may trigger review, throttling, or restriction:

- unusually high request rates
- repeated failed login or token abuse patterns
- bulk record access across unrelated accounts
- repeated export or extraction activity
- heavy automation that exceeds expected clinical or research use
- abuse of AI prompts for unsafe, deceptive, or policy-violating content
- attempts to strip branding, attribution, or licence notices
- derivative deployments marketed as official, sanctioned, or exclusive when they are not

## 6. Resource Boundaries

The platform may apply limits on:

- session length and context size
- file upload size and count
- API requests per minute, hour, or day
- concurrent actions and background jobs
- export volume and frequency
- AI-heavy tasks such as summarization, drafting, or high-cost inference

These limits may vary by role, deployment, and service tier.

## 7. Clinical Safety Expectations

Because PsyPyrus operates in a mental-health setting:

- AI must remain assistive, not authoritative
- crisis content must trigger appropriate safety pathways
- clinicians remain responsible for final judgment and sign-off
- supervision, referral, and escalation workflows must remain functional
- any workflow involving minors, vulnerable adults, or high-risk cases must retain stricter access control and auditability

## 8. Enforcement

The project may respond to misuse by:

- warning the user
- reducing rate limits
- disabling high-cost features
- blocking abusive endpoints or automation
- requiring manual review
- suspending or terminating access
- requesting takedown or remediation for unauthorized derivative deployments

## 9. Mission-Aligned Use

Mission-aligned use emphasizes:

- equitable access
- clinician dignity and workload reduction
- interoperability across settings
- public-benefit deployments
- supervision and referral support
- transparent, auditable care pathways
- low-cost and low-infrastructure operation where possible

## 10. Policy Update Rule

This policy may be updated as the repository, deployment model, legal posture, or mission scope changes.
